Case 1 · Michelin Tire Taiwan

TYREPLUS MARS dealer ERP system rollout

Taiwan deployment of Michelin's global ERP programme on Microsoft Dynamics NAV, delivered with Michelin's IS team in France and the system integrator. Dealers ran different systems with different staffing. Owners worried about cost and data sharing, and staff worried about extra work and new skills. I took over the requirements still open partway through the project and carried out the change work with dealers.

Fit-gap and data

  • Fit-gap analysis between current dealer operations and the target platform.
  • Turned Taiwan-specific regulatory, language and finance needs (Chinese-language display, tax invoice rules, post-dated cheques) into system specifications.
  • Standardized 15 master-data categories, and cleansed and mapped about 50,000 product, customer, supplier and pricing records for migration.

UAT

  • 155 test cases run by an 8-person cross-functional team across sales, finance and service booking.
  • Acceptance threshold: zero S1 and zero S2 defects. Defects logged and tracked to closure in Jira.
  • Readiness assessed across 43 dealer locations in 7 regions to choose pilot stores and set the two-wave rollout order.

Pilot outcome

2 live 1 deferred

3 pilot stores completed training. Slow service was met with on-site support. Interface complaints were tracked for three months and reported to the product team. Owner concerns about old systems led to planned usage incentives.

After launch

  • Monthly dashboard of 8 metrics per store (revenue, gross margin, tyre and non-tyre mix, brand share, footfall, average transaction value, service revenue, share of transactions processed in the system), run for 8 consecutive months across 43 stores.
  • Reconciled MARS data against the legacy JDE channel to find stores that adopted the system on paper but were not transacting in it, then recommended where coaching and resources should go.
  • Checked system use and data quality against audit criteria to find problems in use and gaps in the data.
  • A three-month incentive programme, with results reviewed each month.
